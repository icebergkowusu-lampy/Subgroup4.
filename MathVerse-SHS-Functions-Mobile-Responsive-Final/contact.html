Quick help

My progress disappeared

Success criteria, reflections and assessment scores are saved with your browser's local storage. Clearing browser data, or opening the site in a different browser or private/incognito window, will reset them.

The interactive graph looks empty

Try resizing the window or rotating your device; the canvas redraws automatically. If it still looks blank, refresh the page.

GeoGebra / video won't load

These need an internet connection. Use the backup links provided on the Interactive Lab page to open them directly.

I can't submit the assessment

The submit button unlocks only once every one of the 10 questions has an answer selected or typed in.
